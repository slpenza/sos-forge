# v0.8.9 — Living Longer and Earthquakes This Week, and legend ticks that stay readable

Two datasets for the gallery, the rest of the candidates Steve picked: life expectancy in every country since 1950,
and the past week's earthquakes. Building the first found an old bug in the legend's ticks, fixed here.

## What it decides

**Living Longer: life expectancy at birth in every country, 1950 to 2023.**
- **The data:** Our World in Data's series, with its citation: Riley (2005), Zijdeman et al. (2015), HMD (2025) and
  UN WPP (2024), processed by Our World in Data, CC BY 4.0. It comes through the existing `owid` source.
- **Why 1950:** it's the first year all 236 countries have an estimate. In 1900 only 45 do, and 4 in 1800, so starting
  earlier would show a grey world that fills in all at once in 1950.
- **The look:** countries shaded from 25 to 85 years on the `sea` ramp, dark blue to pale green, over a dimmed relief
  basemap, with a legend.
- **Like Getting Richer:** a 30-second movie, 900 frames at 30 fps, at 15 and 73 seconds too (5 and 1 years a
  second), each painted and with SOS labels.

**Earthquakes This Week: every earthquake of magnitude 4.5 or more in the 7 days before the build.**
- **The data:** the USGS's summary feed, refreshed every minute and cached here for 10 minutes. A US Government work,
  in the public domain, credited to the USGS.
- **Why M4.5:** it's the size seismographs catch everywhere. Below it the USGS records quakes reliably mainly near
  dense networks: its M2.5 feed held 343 quakes, mostly in and around the US. That would make the US look shakier than
  it is.
- **The source, `usgs_quakes`:** it reads any summary feed (`4.5_week`, `significant_month`, …) as points.
  - columns: magnitude, depth, place and time;
  - a `strength` of 10^((mag − 4.5) / 2), so one step up in magnitude glows about three times as bright;
  - quarry blasts and events without a magnitude are left out.
- **What the HUD gets:** the feed's window as `quakes_from` and `quakes_to`, and its strongest magnitude as
  `strongest`.
- **The look:** gold glows over the relief and bathymetry basemap, so the trenches and ridges they line up along show.
  Named with its date, like the aurora and the fires.

**Point sources can hand numbers to the HUD,** as grid sources already did, so the subtitle reads "88 of magnitude
4.5+ to 25 Nov, strongest 6.8" from the data.

**The legend's linear ticks are round and at most seven.**
- **The bug:** the tick step only tried up to ten times the base step. For a range of 60 years that wasn't enough, so
  it fell back to a step of 1 and wrote 61 labels into one smear.
- **The fix:** it now tries steps up to a hundred times the base and allows up to six intervals, so 25 to 85 reads
  30, 40, … 80. A top tick lost to rounding (1 ÷ 0.2 = 4.999…) is kept.
- **Getting Richer** uses a log scale, which this doesn't touch.

## For visitors

- New dataset: Living Longer, life expectancy in every country from 1950 to 2023.
- New dataset: Earthquakes This Week, every earthquake of magnitude 4.5 or more in a week, named with its date.

## What it does not decide

- **Life expectancy before 1950,** too sparse for a whole-world map.
- **Colour by depth.** The points layer has no legend for categories, so every quake is one colour.
- **Refreshing the earthquakes.** The published image is one week's, until it's rebuilt.

## Tested

- **`tests/test_v3_sources.py::test_usgs_reads_quakes`,** with a stubbed feed:
  - the feed is read once, then from the cache;
  - only earthquakes with a magnitude are kept, with their depth and strength;
  - the 7-day window and the strongest quake are reported;
  - an unknown feed, an empty one and a document that isn't a feed are refused.
- **`tests/test_v3_layers.py`:**
  - `test_points_pass_source_times` now checks a source's numbers reach the HUD too;
  - `test_regions_shade_by_year_with_legend` checks 25 to 85 ticks every 10, every range up to 12,345 stays at seven
    ticks or fewer, and 0 to 1 keeps its top tick.
- **By hand, Living Longer:**
  - 1950 and 2023 frames looked at whole;
  - the legend looked at once its ticks were fixed;
  - every length and style built, with the checks below.
- **By hand, Earthquakes This Week:**
  - the live feed held 88 earthquakes, the strongest M6.8, and every check passed;
  - the glows were brightened and the background dimmed once, after the first build was too faint to read.
- **The suite:** 201 Python tests (with the browser tests required), 27 node tests and 26 infra tests pass.
- **Not tested before release:** the gallery cards, which need the zips on the release first.
