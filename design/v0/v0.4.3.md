# v0.4.3 — The simulator keeps the last frame's caption

The simulator's caption went blank at the end of every movie. On a dataset that holds its last frame (`lastdwell`),
it stayed blank for the whole hold, exactly while a presenter talks over the final year.

## What it decides

**`labels.txt` is split without its trailing blank line.**
- A labels file ends with a newline, so splitting it gave one entry more than there are frames, and the last entry
  was empty.
- At the end of a movie, `showCaption` clamps its index to the last entry. It landed on the empty one and hid the
  caption, at the loop on a movie with no hold and for the whole hold on one with `lastdwell`.
- `parseLabels` in `site/js/playlist.js` splits the file, accepting `\r\n` too, and drops trailing blank lines. The
  simulator uses it for both a published dataset and a dropped-in folder.
- Blank lines in the middle are kept, because a frame may have no label.
- The gallery's flat-preview captions were already safe: `labelCues` skips blank runs.

**The browser test reproduces it on any machine.**
- **How CI found it:** in the test it ran the day before, v0.4.2's caption check failed on GitHub's runner and
  passed locally. The 1-second test movie had no hold, so the blank lasted less than a frame. CI's slower machine
  rendered it, and a fast one didn't.
- **The fixture now holds:** the test movie holds its last frame for 3 s (`lastdwell = 3000`), as SOS datasets can.
- **The new check:** after the drag, the test seeks to the end, and a `MutationObserver` records every moment the
  caption is hidden or empty, however brief.
- Against the old simulator it fails, with the same error CI hit. Against the fix it passes.

## What it does not decide

- **What SOS itself does at the end.** Real SOS draws `labels.txt` on the sphere. Whether its last frame shows a
  label there is untested, as before.

## Tested

- **Node:** 12 tests, including `parseLabels`:
  - a trailing newline, `\r\n` endings and an empty file;
  - a blank line kept in the middle.
- **`pytest`:** every test passes, 10 of them in headless Google Chrome. The simulator test passes three runs out of
  three against the fix and fails against the old code.
- **Not yet tested:** the CI browser job on this version, and the live simulator after the deploy.
