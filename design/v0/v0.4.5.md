# v0.4.5 — One help page answers "can I use my own data?"

The main job, making a dataset from your own data, was spread over four pages, and none of them answered it whole.
Asked "can i add my own dataset?", Help's Ask gave four partial answers that largely repeated each other.

## What it decides

**`docs/getting-started.md` owns the job.** Its "Make your own" and "Build it full size" sections become one
section, "Make a dataset from your own data".
- **The three kinds of data that need no code, in a table:**
  - places, from a CSV with `lat` and `lon`;
  - a value per country or per region of your own, from a CSV of code, year and value;
  - GPS tracks, from GPX, TCX or FIT files.
- **Six steps, each linking to the page with the detail:**
  1. start a recipe;
  2. put your data in;
  3. choose what it shows;
  4. credit your source;
  5. check it in the simulator;
  6. build it full size and copy it to the sphere.
- **Regions, which no page showed how to make from your own data:**
  - the `regions` layer that replaces `sos new`'s points layer;
  - the `geojson_regions` line for polygons of your own;
  - the HUD subtitle to change, because the template's `{count} places` has nothing to count and stops the build.
- **One line on what it isn't:** your dataset stays on your computer and your sphere, and isn't added to this
  site's gallery.
- **The intro** now says the page ends with a dataset made from your own data.

**The other pages keep their detail.** The new section links to them rather than repeating them, so nothing
exists in two copies that can drift apart.

## For visitors

- Help: one page walks through making a dataset from your own places, regions or GPS tracks.

## What it does not decide

- **What Help's Ask shows.**
  - **The other pages still answer:** they cover parts of the job, and Ask & Answer reads every page, so it still
    gives several answers. One of them is now complete.
  - **Combining them:** one answer that cites each page is Ask & Answer's change, briefed to that project on
    2026-11-24.
- **When Ask sees this page.** The Help pages are rebuilt by this release, but the corpus Ask answers from is
  uploaded by the Ask & Answer deployment, which reads this repo's checkout. Ask answers from the new page after
  that deployment next runs.
- **A `sos new` template per kind of data.** The template stays a points recipe, and the doc shows the swap.
