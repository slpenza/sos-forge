# v0.9.1 — A NOAA submission states each source's licence

`sos admin`'s draft gave NOAA the credits but not what each source lets anyone do with its data. NOAA would pass these
files to every sphere, so the email should say. Most datasets' READMEs say only "See credits.", because only
OpenStreetMap and CC BY-SA sources add a licence note at build time.

## What it decides

**A Licences section,** after Credits and before Files.
- **One line per source:** its name, then its terms, in the words `docs/data-sources.md` checked them in. A source
  that two credits share, such as two NASA backgrounds, gets one line.
- **Then a link** to `docs/data-sources.md`, which lists every source with its terms.

**Where the terms come from:** `TERMS` in `sosforge/noaa.py`, matched against each credit line by a phrase that
source always writes.
- **Why not the READMEs:** they'd need every dataset rebuilt and republished. The credits are already in the live
  `datasets.json`, which is what the draft reads.
- **Why not `site.toml`:** the terms belong with the public list of sources, not with the private deployment.
- **A credit no source matches** gets a `[[Terms of use for: ...]]` line, and so does a dataset with no credits. The
  page won't send while a `[[...]]` line is left, so a new source's terms are written before a submission goes.

**The wording, per source:**
- **NASA's backgrounds (GIBS) and Scientific Visualization Studio imagery:** not copyrighted; NASA asks to be
  credited.
- **OpenStreetMap:** ODbL 1.0; the image is a Produced Work and keeps the credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors".
- **Natural Earth:** public domain.
- **Our World in Data:** CC BY 4.0. The data it republishes keeps its original source's terms, and both are cited.
  This follows Our World in Data's own policy, rather than claiming CC BY 4.0 for every source behind a chart.
- **GHSL (European Commission JRC):** CC BY 4.0, © European Union; cite the dataset and its DOI.
- **USGS and NOAA SWPC:** US Government works, in the public domain, with their credit.
- **NASA FIRMS:** NASA data, free and open; acknowledge FIRMS and NASA's ESDIS, with a link.

**Smaller changes:**
- `sources/regions.py` names its two credit strings, `NATURAL_EARTH_CREDIT` and `OWID_CREDIT`, so the terms can be
  tested against them.
- The README and `docs/data-sources.md` say where the draft's terms come from.

## What it does not decide

- **The READMEs' licence lines.** They change only when a dataset is rebuilt.
- **Which datasets to submit.** Fires Burning Now, Earthquakes This Week and Aurora Forecast are snapshots of "now".
  Whether they go to a catalog, and under what title, is a separate decision.
- **Reaching the page.** sos-site pins sos-forge, so `sos admin` run from sos-site drafts this way only once
  sos-site's `forge.lock.json` moves to this tag.
