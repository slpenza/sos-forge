# v0.4.6 — Lunar Phases joins the gallery

The bundled Lunar Phases recipe was the first dataset sos-forge made, and the gallery didn't offer it. Spheres whose
library has no moon-phase movie had no way to get one from the site.

## What it decides

**The gallery publishes Lunar Phases, at four lengths and in both text styles.**
- **Lengths:** the standard 24 s, plus 15 s, 59 s and 118 s. The paces are 0.5, 2 and 4 seconds a day, and the
  standard length works out to about 0.8.
  - A presenter talking through each phase can pick a slow version instead of pausing.
- **The pace's step:** `per = { unit = "day", count = 29.5 }`. The movie has no dates for the build to count years
  from, so the step is given in `site.toml`, as it is for Where the World Plays.
- **SOS labels:** at every length, so the phase name, day and percentage lit can stay put while the Moon turns.
- **Data line:** links to NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio CGI Moon Kit, which the recipe's credits already
  cite.

**Its name is "Lunar Phases".** The recipe called it "Lunar Phases (29.5-Day Synodic Cycle)". Length versions add
their own brackets, which gave names like "Lunar Phases (29.5-Day Synodic Cycle) (15 s)". The description still
says it covers a 29.5-day cycle.

**It is not headed for NOAA's catalog.** The SOS Data Catalog has had a Moon Phases movie since 9 Oct 2012,
credited to NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio and LRO. This one is offered on the site for spheres that want
the labelled, multi-length version.

## For visitors

- New dataset: Lunar Phases: the Sun circling the tidally locked Moon over 29.5 days, at 15 s, 24 s, 59 s and
  1 min 58 s, each also with SOS labels.

## What it does not decide

- **Lunar Phases' look.** The recipe's layers, HUD and checks are unchanged.
- **Other bundled recipes:**
  - Pinball Map still needs its token.
  - My Activities is personal data.

## Tested

- **The builds:** all eight versions (four lengths, two text styles), each in MPEG-4 Part 2 and H.264, built at
  4096 × 2048 with every check passing. Each playlist is named from the new name, such as "Lunar Phases (59 s, SOS
  labels)".
- **The bundle:** `sos bundle lunar_phases` wrote 16 zips and 7 browser previews, 2.87 GB in all, for data-2026-11.
- **The suite:** 151 tests pass on the tree as committed.
- **The site, built locally from `dist/`:**
  - the card offers 15 s, 24 s, 59 s and 1 min 58 s, at 0.5, 0.8, 2 and 4 s a day, and both text styles;
  - choosing 59 s with SOS labels points the simulator at that version and Download at
    `lunar_phases_59s_labels_4k.zip`, and plays its 2048-wide preview;
  - the simulator opens on that version;
  - no page errors and no sideways scroll at 1280 px and 390 px.
- **Not tested here:** the build against the release. The tag's release run checks every zip is on data-2026-11,
  after they are uploaded.
