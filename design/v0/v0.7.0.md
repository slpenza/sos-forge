# v0.7.0 — Anonymous visit and download counts, and sos-forge's row on stevenpenza.com's dashboard

stevenpenza.com's dashboard gave SOS Forge a row in its v0.7.14, with nothing to show: sos-forge published no
status document. Steve asked for visits and downloads, and for a bar chart worth having. The site kept no counts at
all. GitHub's download counts for the release zips include sos-forge's own builds and smoke checks, which fetch them
on every release.

## What it decides

**The site counts page views and Download clicks, anonymously.**
- **The endpoint:** `POST /api/hit`, on the site's own origin through CloudFront, as `/api/suggest` is.
- **What a page sends:**
  - one hit per view, `{kind: "view", page, entry}`, where `page` is `<body data-page>` (home, simulator, changes or
    suggest);
  - `entry` says whether the visit began there: there was no referrer, or it was another site;
  - one hit per click on a dataset's Download, `{kind: "download", dataset, version}`. The version is `standard`,
    `length` or `labels` (`versionKind` in versions.js).
- **What a page doesn't use:** a cookie, storage or an identifier.
- **When nothing is sent:**
  - the browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control;
  - `site.toml`'s `[site] count` is false. It is false unless set, so forks and test builds stay quiet. The live site
    sets it.
- **What's counted:** daily totals in a DynamoDB table, keyed by New York day:
  - visits;
  - views per page;
  - downloads per version and per dataset;
  - suggestions sent;
  - an all-time downloads total.
  Daily rows expire after 400 days. The table is kept if the stack is ever replaced.
- **One client can't inflate the counts:**
  - at most 200 hits an hour from one address count, tracked as a keyed hash in the limits table, which expires
    within a day;
  - the API takes 20 hits a second, in bursts of 50.
- **Turned down:**
  - CloudFront access logs, which keep visitors' addresses;
  - GitHub's download counts, which count sos-forge's own fetches.

**Each route has its own throttle.** The stage allows 20 a second in bursts of 50. `POST /api/suggest`, which sends
mail, keeps 1 a second in bursts of 5.

**Help isn't counted.** Its pages are built by Ask & Answer, and the head fragment sos-forge gives them can't carry a
script. A visit that begins on Help is counted when it reaches a page that counts, but not as a visit.

**Every five minutes, a Lambda writes `status/sos.json` into stevenpenza.com's private dashboard bucket.**
- **Where it writes:** the bucket's name comes from the production environment's variable `SOS_STATUS_BUCKET`,
  passed to CDK as `SOSFORGE_STATUS_BUCKET`, so it never enters the repo. Without it the publisher isn't built.
- **What it may touch:**
  - `BatchGetItem` on the counts table;
  - `GetObject` on the site bucket's `datasets.json`;
  - `PutObject` on that one key, `status/sos.json`.
- **The document,** in the form Placard Maker's uses:
  - `metrics`:
    - `visits_today` and `views_today`;
    - `downloads_30d`, and its three parts: `downloads_standard_30d`, `downloads_lengths_30d`, `downloads_labels_30d`;
    - `downloads_all`, `suggestions_30d`;
    - `noaa_catalog`: how many published datasets have a NOAA catalog link;
  - `bars`: 30-day downloads as `[standard, other lengths, SOS labels]`, the meter's three segments;
  - `spark` and `spark_days`: visits per New York day for 14 days, starting from 24 Nov 2026, when counting began.
- **Honest omission:** a read that fails writes nothing. The document ages out, and the row shows NO DATA rather than
  a guess.
- **"All time"** means since counting began.

**The row on stevenpenza.com is that site's to lay out.** Steve agreed on:
- **Slots:** VISITS TODAY, DOWNLOADS 30D, DOWNLOADS ALL TIME, IN NOAA CATALOG. `suggestions_30d` is there if a slot
  is wanted for it.
- **Meter:** downloads 30d by version.
- **Trend:** VISITS / DAY.
Adding `sos` to its metrics function's systems, and the row's markup, are its change.

**Pages say so.** The home page, What's new, the suggestion form and the simulator's panel each say that page views and
downloads are counted without cookies, and that the network address is kept only as a one-way code for less than a
day.

**The home page's What's new moves below the gallery.** The datasets come first, after the introduction. The latest
three lines and the link to the page now follow them.

## What it does not decide

- **Who may see the figures signed out.** That is the dashboard's `PUBLIC_FIELDS`, as for Placard Maker's.
- **A per-dataset view of downloads.** The counts are kept per dataset (`dlset`), but the row shows versions.

## Tested

- **`infra/test/metrics.test.mjs`, 6 tests:**
  - New York days and day ranges;
  - which counts a hit adds, and that malformed hits add none;
  - the counter answers 204 to everything, counts only well-formed hits, and keeps the all-time total without an
    expiry;
  - one client capped per hour, with no address in any key;
  - the status document's every figure, the meter, a trend that starts on the day counting began, and the 30 days
    of keys it reads;
  - a failed read of the counts or the manifest writes nothing.
- **`infra/test/suggest.test.mjs`:** a sent suggestion is counted once, and a failed count doesn't fail it.
- **`infra/test/site-stack.test.mjs`:**
  - both routes, with the stage's throttle and the suggestion route's own;
  - the counts table is retained and has TTL;
  - the counter's environment;
  - no status publisher without a bucket; with one, a 5-minute schedule and permissions limited to one key and the
    manifest.
- **`site/js/count.test.mjs`:** a visit begins from nowhere or another site; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control
  turn counting off; a version's kind is one the counter knows.
- **`test_browser.py::test_counts_views_and_downloads`,** against a stand-in counter:
  - nothing is sent while `config.json` leaves counting off;
  - with it on:
    - the home page sends a view that begins a visit;
    - a labels-version Download sends its dataset and version;
    - the simulator, reached from the home page, sends a view that doesn't;
  - no cookie is set.
- **`test_site.py`:** `config.json` carries `count`, and the setting must be true or false.
- **The real app, synthesized:**
  - with no bucket, there is no status publisher and no schedule;
  - with one, the publisher and its 5-minute schedule are there;
  - a malformed name stops synthesis.
- **The suite:** 171 Python tests (with the browser tests required), 23 node tests and 21 infra tests pass.
- **Not tested before the deploy:** the AWS ports, and the document arriving in the dashboard's bucket. They need the
  variable set and a release.
