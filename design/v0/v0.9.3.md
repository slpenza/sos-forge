# v0.9.3 — The home page's question field and Ask button are a .btn's height, so Ask matches Download

v0.9.2 drew Ask as the site's `.btn`: the same corners, fill, border, colour, font and spacing as Download and Read
the help. It kept Ask & Answer's 46 px height, though, so Ask and the field stood 6 px taller than the buttons beside
and below them, and Ask still didn't look like Download.

## What it decides

- **One height for the row:** the question field and Ask take a `.btn`'s rendered height, 40 px, in place of
  Ask & Answer's 46 px floor. A `.btn` has a 36 px floor, which its line of text grows to 40 px.
- **How:** one rule in `site/css/site.css`, `min-height: 40px` on `.cta .ask-box .ask-boxfield` and
  `.cta .ask-box .ask-boxbtn`. Like v0.9.2's rule, it targets Ask & Answer's classes by name.
- **Every width:** the row is 40 px tall at 1280, 375 and 320 px wide, with no sideways scroll. On a phone, the field
  and Ask wrap below Read the help, all at the same height.

## What it does not decide

- **The question field's corners.** They stay square, as Ask & Answer draws them.
- **Help's own Ask panel.** It keeps Ask & Answer's sizes. Only the home page's row changes.
- **What's new.** This is a layout change, so the version has no For visitors lines.

## Tested

- **`test_browser.py::test_home_ask_box_hands_the_question_to_help`** now also checks that Read the help, the field
  and Ask have one height. It fails without the rule, measuring 40, 46 and 46 px. The style comparison from v0.9.2 is
  unchanged.
- **The full suite:** 202 tests pass.
- **By hand:** the rule injected into the live home page in Chrome measured 40 px for all three controls at 1280, 375
  and 320 px, with no sideways scroll.
