# v0.6.1 — A source without its owner's permission leaves, suggestions can go to a test address, and smaller fixes

Four changes Steve asked for:
- **A source without permission:** sos-forge named a third-party source that it has no permission to use. Its API
  needs a token approved by hand, and none was granted. A public listing reads as using someone's data without
  asking.
- **A test address:** v0.6.0 sends every suggestion to `sos@stevenpenza.com`, where it is stored with NOAA's
  correspondence and raises his notice. He wants to try the form against one of his own addresses first, and his
  personal address doesn't belong in a public repo.
- **Wider boxes:** the two question boxes, on the home page and on Help, should be half as wide again.
- **What's new:** formatting changes don't belong in it.

## What it decides

**A source is named publicly only once its owner has said yes.**
- **What leaves:**
  - that source's bundled recipe, its source module and their two tests;
  - its row in the README, `docs/data-sources.md` and the recipe reference;
  - its troubleshooting section, and its line in `.env.example`.
- **The old scope docs:** v0.1.0 and v0.4.6 no longer name it. v0.1.0 keeps a line saying a fourth recipe was
  taken out, and why.
- **The live Help** answers from what this release uploads, so it stops naming it too.
- **What can't change:** git history and v0.4.6's release notes on GitHub. History is never rewritten. The release
  notes can be edited only with Steve's word.
- **If permission comes:** the recipe and module come back from history in their own version, with the owner's
  terms in the scope doc.
- **The rule, for every source:** check its terms before it is named anywhere public. One that needs permission,
  or a key approved by hand, isn't named until the permission arrives.

**Where suggestions go is a deploy setting, `SOSFORGE_SUGGEST_TO`, defaulting to `sos@stevenpenza.com`.**
- **Where it comes from:** `release.yml` passes the production environment's variable `SOS_SUGGEST_TO` to the
  release. `infra/bin/app.mjs` reads it, and uses sos@ when it is empty or missing.
- **What follows it:** both the Lambda's `TO_ADDRESS` and its IAM condition on `ses:Recipients`. The Lambda can
  still send to exactly one address, whichever is set.
- **A wrong value stops the deploy:** anything that isn't an email address fails synthesis, before anything
  changes.
- **Switching:**
  - **to a test address:** set the variable, then release;
  - **back to sos@:** delete the variable, then release.
- **While it points at a test address:**
  - suggestions arrive there directly, as plain email from `sos-suggest@stevenpenza.com`, with Reply-To set to the
    visitor when they gave an email;
  - they aren't stored under `mail/sos/`;
  - they don't raise the stevenpenza.com notice, and they don't show on `sos admin`.
- **Turned down:** a test address written in `app.mjs`. The repo is public.

**Both question boxes are 720 px, up from 480.**
- **Where it's set:** the same cap as v0.5.4, in `site/css/site.css` (`.cta .home-ask`) and in
  `site/help-head.html` (`.ask-askform`).
- **On the home page,** the 820 px cap moves from the whole introduction to its heading and paragraph:
  - the row of buttons and the box may use the page's width, up to 1280 px;
  - from about 1,180 px the two buttons and the 720 px box share one row;
  - narrower than that, the box wraps under the buttons rather than shrinking;
  - the text keeps its reading width.
- **On a phone:** both fill the row, as before.

**What's new leaves out formatting.**
- **What counts as formatting:** text sizes, spacing, colours and widths.
- **What goes:**
  - v0.5.3's and v0.5.4's For visitors sections, which were nothing else;
  - v0.4.9's "answers use the page's width";
  - v0.4.10's "the answer panel no longer sits in a box".
- **The rule** is in the README, beside the section's form, for the scope docs to come.
- **Not formatting:** v0.4.7's new wording for the Text and Length choices stays.
- **This version** has no For visitors section: a deploy setting and two widths.

## What it does not decide

- **Pausing the form.** It stays live. Until a release carries the variable, a visitor's suggestion goes to sos@,
  as v0.6.0 does.

## Tested

- **The real app, `node bin/app.mjs`, synthesized three ways:**
  - **no setting:** the Lambda's `TO_ADDRESS` and its `ses:Recipients` condition are `sos@stevenpenza.com`;
  - **`test@example.org`:** both are `test@example.org`;
  - **"not an address":** synthesis stops with "SOSFORGE_SUGGEST_TO is not an email address".
- **The boxes, on a local build:**
  - both measure 366 px at 390 px, and 720 px at 768, 1024 and 1440 px;
  - the home page's box shares the buttons' row at 1180, 1280, 1440 and 1920 px, and wraps at 1024 px and below;
  - the introduction's paragraph keeps its 820 px cap;
  - no sideways scroll. `test_home_ask_box_hands_the_question_to_help` now expects 720 px for both.
- **What's new, from this repo's tags:** v0.5.3 and v0.5.4 no longer appear; v0.4.9 and v0.4.10 keep one line each,
  without their formatting.
- **Nothing in the tree names the source,** checked with `git grep`. The three remaining mentions of "pinball"
  are Steve's own Pinball project.
- **The suite:** 170 Python tests (with the browser tests required; two went with the removed source), 20 node tests and 13 infra tests pass.
