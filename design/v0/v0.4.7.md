# v0.4.7 — The simulator draws SOS labels on the sphere, and the picker reads plainly

The simulator showed a dataset's SOS labels as a caption at the top of the view, which no sphere does. The Text
select called them "fixed on screen", but a sphere has no screen, and the pace read "4 s a sport".

## What it decides

**The simulator draws SOS labels where the sphere draws them.**
- **What NOAA's playlist reference says:**
  - the label is drawn four times around the sphere, centred `labelvertical` degrees north and `labelhorizontal`
    degrees east "of the center of each projector", so in the room's frame;
  - the defaults are −25° and 0°;
  - `labelheight` is in degrees of latitude, with the width computed from the text, and no default is given;
  - with no `labelformat`, the text is white Free Sans on a clear background.
- **How the simulator follows it:**
  - four copies, 90° apart, from `labelhorizontal`, at `labelvertical`, read from the version's playlist;
  - each copy runs along its centre's parallel, `labelheight` tall, or 4° when the playlist doesn't say;
  - white Free Sans (or Helvetica or Arial when it isn't installed) on a clear background;
  - the copies stay put while the picture spins or tilts, and move with the sphere when the view is dragged.
- **Shown or hidden as the playlist says:**
  - the SOS-labels versions start with them on;
  - the painted versions start with them off, because their playlists ship them hidden;
  - a dropped folder follows its `labelvisible`, and shows none when its playlist has no `label` line.
- **The Caption button becomes Labels,** which switches them as the iPad's Display Elements list does. It is
  disabled for a dataset without labels.
- **For screen readers:** the current line is kept in a hidden text element.
- **Turned down:** keeping the caption beside the labels. A text overlay no sphere shows is what made the old
  version misleading.

**The picker's words.**
- **Text:** "Painted into the picture" and "SOS labels, fixed on the sphere". The old pair, "on the globe" and "on
  screen", hid the real difference: painted text turns with the picture, and labels don't.
- **Pace:** "4 s/sport", "0.5 s/year", "0.8 s/day".
  - "4 sec/sport" was turned down: the length beside it is "1 min 58 s", so one option would spell seconds two ways.
  - Spelling the lengths "sec" too would rename every playlist and mean rebuilding every published dataset.

## What it does not decide

- **The labels' real size and look on a given sphere:**
  - NOAA gives `labelheight` no default;
  - a site can restyle labels in NOAA's Label Editor.
  The simulator's doc says so. Writing `labelheight` into every playlist would pin the size on both, but that is a
  rebuild of every dataset, left for later.
- **The gallery's flat preview.** It keeps the labels as native captions, because a flat video has no sphere to
  draw them on.
- **`labelposition`,** the form before SOS 5.3, which is deprecated and isn't read.

## Tested

- **`playlist.test.mjs`:** the label settings, from no playlist lines (−25°, 0°, 4°, shown), from `labelvisible =
  false`, and from out-of-range values, which are clamped.
- **`lengths.test.mjs` and `versions.test.mjs`:** the pace as "0.5 s/year" and "4 s/sport".
- **`test_browser.py`:**
  - **the card:** the new Text wording and the pace in the Length options;
  - **`test_simulator_labels_on_the_sphere`,** comparing the sphere drawn with labels on and off:
    - the painted version starts with labels off, the SOS-labels one with them on, and they follow the frames;
    - the label lands on the sphere's front, south of centre, wider than tall;
    - after 15° of spin the picture has turned but the label's centre hasn't moved;
    - dragging the view moves the label with the sphere;
    - at the end of the movie the last frame's line stays, with no blank moment;
  - **`test_simulator_switches_versions`:** switching to SOS labels turns them on and shows that version's line
    from the same place;
  - three runs out of three pass.
- **The suite:** 151 Python tests and 16 node tests pass.
- **The site, built locally from the real datasets:**
  - Lunar Phases and Getting Richer with SOS labels draw them along the parallel at 25° south, curving with the
    sphere in Orbit and at eye level in Visitor;
  - Getting Richer's painted version starts with them off;
  - no page errors, and no sideways scroll at 1280 px or 390 px.
  - On a phone the labels are small, at their true size against the sphere; pinching steps closer.
