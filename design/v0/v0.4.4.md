# v0.4.4 — Pick a version once: it plays, opens in the simulator and downloads

Each movie card offered its lengths and text styles as two rows of chips: pace chips with the total beside them,
then Painted and SOS labels beside the buttons. It read as busy, and the choice only set what Download fetched. The
preview and the simulator always played the standard length with painted text, so a presenter couldn't rehearse at
the length they would show.

## What it decides

**A card has Length and Text selects above its buttons, and they drive the whole card.**
- **Length:** each length with its pace, such as "1 min 41 s · 0.5 s a year".
- **Text:** "Painted on the globe" or "SOS labels, fixed on screen".
- **What they set:**
  - **Open in simulator** and the poster link, as `simulator.html?d=<slug>&s=<seconds>&text=labels`. The standard
    length and painted text are left out of the address.
  - **Download**, as before.
  - **The poster's play button:** it plays the chosen version. Changing the version while a preview plays swaps it
    in at the same point.
- **A card without a choice:** a movie with one version, or a still, keeps a plain Download link and a length or
  "Still image" pill, and shows no selects.
- **The pills:** one neutral outlined style for the date and length pills. A movie with selects shows only its date
  pill, since the select shows the length.
- **What went:** the chip rows, the "N s in all" text, `chips()` and `paceShort`.
- **Turned down:**
  - Hiding the choices in a Download panel looked tidy, but it hid what the simulator would open.
  - Playing the standard preview at another speed (`playbackRate`) gets the timing right, but at 0.2× it plays at
    about 6 frames a second.

**The simulator shows the same selects and switches in place.**
- Under **Dataset**, for a published movie with more than one version.
- Switching keeps the place in the movie, as a fraction of its length, since lengths differ. It also keeps it
  paused or playing.
- The address follows the choice, and the download buttons show that version's file.
- The SOS-labels version plays without panels in the picture, with its labels as the fixed caption, which is what
  the sphere shows.
- **The picker is shared:** `site/js/versions.js` holds it for the cards and the simulator, with
  `pickVersion`, `simulatorHref` and `lengthOption` as pure functions.

**Every version gets a browser preview, made once and kept on the data release.**
- **Why:** browsers can't play MPEG-4 Part 2, and the extra lengths and SOS-labels versions are published only in
  that format, as 4K zips. Fetching those in CI and converting them on every deploy would mean about 1.4 GB of
  downloads.
- **`sos bundle` makes them:** for every version other than the standard one, it also writes `<version>_web.zip`,
  with `preview.mp4`, `labels.txt` and `playlist.sos`.
  - `preview.mp4` is H.264, 2048 wide, with the same settings as the standard preview.
  - It is made from the version's H.264 folder when there is one, and otherwise converted from the MPEG-4.
- **`sos site` publishes them:** each goes under `datasets/<slug>/<version>/`, for example
  `datasets/getting_richer/101s_labels/`.
  - **Where it gets them:** from the zip in `--dist`, else from the built folder, else from the release.
  - **What it refuses:** a preview that isn't H.264.
  - Each `datasets.json` length gains `previews.painted` and `previews.labels`, `{video, labels}`.
  - The download check also requires each `_web.zip`, so a deploy can't link a version it can't play.
- **The data release:** 21 more assets on `data-2026-11`, 69 MB in all. They are 9 extra lengths and 12 SOS-labels
  versions. Making them took about 4 minutes on the iMac.

**Smaller fixes found on the way.**
- **`unzip_folder` clears its folder first.** It extracted into a reused cache folder and returned the first
  `playlist.sos` it found, so leftovers from an earlier zip could win. The new test caught it.
- **The browser tests' server serves byte ranges, as CloudFront does.** Python's server doesn't, and without ranges
  Chrome can't seek a video: it jumps back to the start. v0.4.3's end-of-movie check passed only because the 1-second
  movie reached its end by itself. Its seek to the end now really seeks.
- **The smoke check covers the previews:** it now also requires every version's preview and labels on the deployed
  site to answer (`preview_paths`).

## What it does not decide

- **A fractional place across lengths is approximate for uneven pacing.** It is exact when time maps linearly to
  the data, as every current movie does.
- **The version's own `playlist.sos` settings.** The simulator uses the dataset's (frame rate, holds, tilt), which
  match across versions today.
- **H.264 4K downloads of the extra versions.** They stay MPEG-4 only.

## Tested

- **Node:** 15 tests. New are `versions.test.mjs`:
  - the default version and its link;
  - a picked length and style, with seconds given as a string;
  - fallbacks for an unknown length or style, and for a dataset without lengths;
  - the option wording.
- **`pytest`, `test_site.py::test_version_previews`:**
  - `sos bundle`'s preview zips: their contents, width and codec, from the H.264 folder or converted from MPEG-4;
  - the site build taking each version from its zip, its folder or the release;
  - refusing a non-H.264 preview.
- **Updated:** `test_lengths_and_download_checks` (`version_stems` and the `_web.zip` check) and
  `test_build.py::test_length_variant_build` (bundle output).
- **Headless Google Chrome, 11 tests:**
  - **The card:** the selects set Open, the poster link and Download. A single-version movie and a still have none.
  - **The flat preview:** swaps to the chosen version's video and captions while playing.
  - **The simulator:**
    - opens the version a link names;
    - switching to SOS labels while paused halfway lands on "Both 15", still paused;
    - the address and downloads follow, and a dataset without versions hides the selects.
  - **The smoke lists:** cover every preview, and each one is served.
- **A real build from `dist/`, with screenshots at 1280 px and 390 px:**
  - Getting Richer set to 1 min 41 s with SOS labels links `?d=getting_richer&s=101&text=labels` and
    `getting_richer_101s_labels_4k.zip`.
  - The simulator plays that version with no panels and the caption "1824 · Average income per person".
- **Not yet tested:** the deployed site and CI's browser job on this version. The 21 preview zips must be on the
  data release before the tag, or the deploy's download check stops it.
